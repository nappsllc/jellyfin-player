## Description
JellyPlayer is a simple music player for your own Jellyfin server.

Choose one folder from your library. JellyPlayer lists every song in it, including subfolders, with cover art, title and artist, and starts playing it. It can begin automatically each time you open the app.

• Big cover player with previous, play/pause and next right on the artwork
• Scroll the list and the player shrinks to a mini player. Tap it to bring it back without losing your place
• Search the folder by title, artist or album
• Shuffle, and the folder plays on a loop
• Plays in the background, with lock screen, Control Center, headphone and car controls with covers
• Caches songs and covers so the next track starts right away. Choose a cache size from none up to 100 GB
• Landscape layout with the player next to the list
• Shows how much of the song has loaded, like a video player

JellyPlayer works with Jellyfin and Jellyfin-compatible servers, at home over your local network or anywhere over HTTPS or a VPN. You need your own server and account. JellyPlayer doesn't include any music.

JellyPlayer is not affiliated with the Jellyfin project.
