# JellyPlayer Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 10 November 2026_

JellyPlayer is a music player for your own Jellyfin server. It is made by napps.

**What the app collects:** nothing. JellyPlayer has no accounts of its own, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. The developer receives no data from the app.

**What stays on your phone:**
- The address of your Jellyfin server, your username, and a sign-in token. The token is kept in the iOS Keychain.
- Your settings (chosen folder, auto-play, shuffle, cache size).
- A cache of songs and cover images, so music plays faster and works with a weak connection. You can change its size or turn it off in Settings.

**Who the app talks to:** only the Jellyfin server you enter. Your username, password, and playback requests go straight to that server. That server is run by you or by whoever gave you the account, and its own privacy rules apply.

**Local network:** iOS asks for local network access so the app can reach a server on your home network. It isn't used for anything else.

**Deleting your data:** deleting the app removes everything it stored.

**Contact:** open an issue at https://github.com/nappsllc/jellyfin-player/issues
